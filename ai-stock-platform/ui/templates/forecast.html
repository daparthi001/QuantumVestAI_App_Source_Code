    Bollinger Bands

    Bollinger Bands show volatility and potential price levels. The middle band is a 20-day moving average, while the upper and lower bands represent 2 standard deviations.

    MACD

    MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) helps identify momentum and trend changes. When the MACD line crosses above the signal line, it may indicate a bullish trend.
