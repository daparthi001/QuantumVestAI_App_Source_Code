📚 Understanding Stock Flow Visualizations

Shows real-time price movements as flowing streams. Each line represents a stock's price trajectory over time, with smooth animations highlighting trends and volatility. The flowing nature helps identify momentum patterns and correlation between stocks.

Displays the relationship between stock price and trading volume. Green dots indicate stocks with positive performance, while red dots show declining stocks. The position reveals whether high-volume trading correlates with price movements.

Aggregates performance by sector, showing which industries are leading or lagging. This view helps identify sector rotation patterns and broad market trends that affect related stocks within the same industry.
